The calculation

Let L be the opening length, a the first centre's top offset, b the last centre's bottom offset, and n the number of centres.

spacing = (L − a − b) ÷ (n − 1)
centre i = a + i × spacing, for i = 0 through n − 1.

In the example, (60 − 3 − 3) ÷ (7 − 1) = 9 cm. The centres are 3, 12, 21, 30, 39, 48 and 57 cm from the top. There are six intervals between seven buttons.

Before transferring the marks

Check the pattern's closure instructions. Pin or baste the placement on a sample, consider button diameter, buttonhole length, seam allowances and areas under tension, then remeasure the marks. Round only when marking with your ruler; repeated rounded intervals can accumulate error.
