Grid comparison

What is counted

For each orientation, subtract twice the edge margin from each fabric dimension. Then calculate:

across = floor((usable width + gap) ÷ (piece width + gap))
rows = floor((usable length + gap) ÷ (piece length + gap))
count = across × rows

The extra gap in each numerator prevents charging a gap beyond the last piece. With 140 × 100 cm fabric, a 2 cm margin on every edge, and 20 × 30 cm pieces without gaps, the usable area is 136 × 96 cm. The original grid fits 6 × 3 = 18 pieces. If rotation is permitted, the turned grid fits 4 × 4 = 16 pieces.

Before cutting

Measure usable fabric after any planned prewashing. Confirm the cutting rectangle, grain direction, allowance and motif requirements. A mixed-orientation layout may fit more pieces than these two grids; lay out paper templates before cutting valuable fabric.
