What the percentages mean

Stretch
(stretched − original) ÷ original × 100
Residual growth
(released − original) ÷ original × 100
Extension recovered
(stretched − released) ÷ (stretched − original) × 100

Recovery is undefined when there was no extension. Unexpected readings can produce values outside 0–100%; the tool flags them without hiding the measurement.

Read the result carefully

Error = (measured − intended) ÷ intended × 100. A negative value means the printed side is smaller; a positive value means it is larger.

The tool uses ±0.5% as a comparison threshold, and flags an X/Y difference greater than 0.5 percentage points. These are tool thresholds, not a guarantee of accuracy. Follow your pattern’s stated tolerance.

If the result is off, check the PDF’s instructions, paper size and Actual Size / 100% setting, then print another test page. Different X/Y readings can also reflect measurement uncertainty; repeat the measurement.

These readings do not identify the cause of a printing problem. Do not use them to grade a pattern or resize a garment.
